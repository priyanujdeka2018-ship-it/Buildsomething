Structure (STAR discipline)

Strong: crisp Situation→Task→Action→Result, natural transitions, ≤2 min, no follow-up needed to grasp the outcome. Weak: rambling, missing components, no clear result, >3 min.

Specificity (numbers, names, outcomes)

Strong: 3+ concrete data points - scale, outcome, timeline all quantified. Weak: "a large team," "significant improvement."

Vocabulary (target-role language)

Strong: zero Sobha-internal terms - every concept in {{TARGET}} language. Weak: 2+ untranslated internal terms (see the Vocabulary map in Reference).

Assertiveness (your Low-D development area)

Strong: leads with "I decided / I held / I built / I escalated," zero hedges, short declaratives at the key moment. Weak: "I think," "we sort of," passive voice, analysis before action.
